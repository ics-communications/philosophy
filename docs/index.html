The MA in full

Pursue Your Question in Depth

PhD in Philosophy

A doctorate to pursue an original research question with a Senior Member mentor.

The full-time pathway: about five years

  1. About 2 years Coursework, the literature review and proposal, languages, and the candidacy examination.
  2. About 3 years Dissertation research, writing and oral defence.
The PhD in full

  • Aesthetics, Hermeneutics, and Philosophy of Discourse
  • Anthropology and Ethics
  • History and Historiography of Philosophy
  • Knowledge, Truth, and Learning
  • Philosophy of Religion and Philosophical Theology
  • Social and Political Philosophy
  • Theology and Biblical Studies

Study from where you are

Every MA and PhD seminar in the 2026–27 catalogue meets online, live, at a set weekly time.

Part-time, or studying from abroad? Write to Neal DeRoo nderoo@icscanada.edu
Background

No philosophy degree is needed for the MA; a philosophy background is encouraged but not required for the PhD.

MA questions and answers
Part-time

If you work full-time, take one course a term; you can try a course or two for credit before committing to a program.

Take a course before applying
From abroad
You can study from outside Canada; Toronto and Amsterdam are academic connections, not places you have to live. ICS cannot currently offer student visas, so international students study online. VERIFY Academic Office (current international-student and visa wording) — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION
Cost
ICS keeps tuition affordable. Current fees are on the tuition and fees page.

Apprenticeship, not transaction

Students are Junior Members; faculty are Senior Members. One Senior Member mentors you through the degree.

Scholarship as a Practice of Wisdom

Rigorous thinking answers to life: to persons, churches, institutions and the culture we share.

  • Neal DeRoo

    Professor of Philosophy
    MA/PhD Program Director

    How spiritual forces, not only social and political ones, shape the way we experience the world, and what that means for politics, culture and the church.

  • Nik Ansell

    Associate Professor of Theology

    Biblical and systematic theology that stays connected to lived experience: Christology, eschatology, wisdom, and the theology of gender.

    Has directed 5 MA theses

  • Ronald A. Kuipers

    Professor of Philosophy of Religion

    What faith can mean in a secular, pluralistic age, worked out with pragmatism, hermeneutics and critical theory.

    Has directed 4 PhD dissertations and 14 MA theses
