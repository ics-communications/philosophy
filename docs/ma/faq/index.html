MA questions

Plain answers to the questions people ask most about the MA in Philosophy VERIFY Academic Office (official degree name).

Not answered here? Write to Neal DeRoo, the Program Director.

Is it for me?

Do I need a background in philosophy?

No. The fields include Theology and Biblical Studies, and a large part of the full-time faculty teaches theology and biblical studies rather than philosophy. ICS looks for a four-year degree, ideally with a concentration related to the field you want to study.

Will the MA prepare me for a PhD?

The MA ends in a substantial thesis and an oral defence, the kind of independent work doctoral programs look for, including the ICS PhD.

How you would study

Do I have to move to Toronto?

Every MA and PhD seminar in the 2026–27 catalogue meets online, live, at a set weekly time. Times are Eastern Time (Toronto).

ICS is based in Toronto and affiliated with the Toronto School of Theology, and its conjoint PhD is with the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. These are academic connections, not places you have to live.

Can I study part-time?

Yes. The MA can be completed part-time. If you work full-time, ICS recommends one course a term. You can take a couple of courses for credit before committing to a program, and they can later count toward the degree.

Can I study from outside Canada? What about visas?

Yes. Because every MA and PhD seminar in the 2026–27 catalogue meets online, you can take the programs from outside Canada.

ICS cannot currently offer student visas, so international students study online. VERIFY Academic Office (current international-student and visa wording) — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION

All programs require proficiency in English; ICS’s international students page gives the details.

Can I take a course before applying?

You can take a couple of courses for credit before committing to a program, and they can later count toward the degree. You are welcome to take courses for credit without pursuing a degree. Write to the Academic Registrar for a course registration form.

The degree itself

How long does the MA take?

Two years full-time. Full-time study means six semester courses a year, for example three in the fall and three in the winter. Part-time study takes longer, at a pace you plan with your mentor.

What does the MA curriculum involve?

Ten seminars, three foundation courses, six electives, and one capstone interdisciplinary seminar, plus a language requirement and a thesis. Up to two courses may come from the Toronto School of Theology.

What is the MA thesis?

With your mentor you build a reading list, write a proposal, then write a thesis of 17,000–25,000 words and defend it orally before your thesis committee.

What are the language requirements?

Before the thesis examination you show reading knowledge of one language other than English, relevant to your field or thesis. An approved language course usually meets this and counts as an elective.

Can courses from elsewhere count?

Up to two Toronto School of Theology courses can count toward your degree. ICS accepts up to two credit transfers for courses at the right academic level that fit your program, but not from a program for which you already received a degree.

Applying and cost

What does it cost, and is there financial aid?

ICS keeps tuition affordable. Current fees are on the tuition and fees page.
