Find Your Scholarly Voice

The MA in Philosophy VERIFY Academic Office (official degree name) brings Christian scholarship to questions in philosophy, theology and biblical studies.

Degree
MA in Philosophy
Length
Two years full-time; part-time available
Study
Online seminars, live, in Eastern Time

Who the MA is for

  • Teachers, ministers, writers and leaders seeking disciplined Christian thought for their work.
  • Students preparing for doctoral research.
  • People exploring questions in culture, politics, education, aesthetics or religion.

In small seminars, faith opens questions. You read closely, argue and write with a faculty mentor—called a Senior Member at ICS.

Admission. A four-year bachelor’s degree or its equivalent, preferably with a concentration related to your intended field at ICS, and an overall average of B+.

You need no prior connection to ICS or Reformational philosophy.

Ten seminars and a thesis

3 foundation courses

A shared biblical, philosophical and spiritual frame.

6 electives

Choose your field and courses with your mentor. Up to two courses may be taken at the Toronto School of Theology.

1 interdisciplinary capstone

Bring your research to a shared question in contemporary culture.

Thesis and defence

With your mentor, develop a reading list and proposal, write a thesis of 17,000–25,000 words, then defend it orally.

Plan your pace

Full-time students take foundations and electives in year one; complete coursework and the thesis in year two. Your mentor helps plan a part-time sequence.

A full-time load is usually three courses a term. If you work full-time, ICS recommends one.

Fields, language and transfer credit

Fields of study

Choose an interdisciplinary field with your mentor:

  • Aesthetics, Hermeneutics, and Philosophy of Discourse
  • Anthropology and Ethics
  • History and Historiography of Philosophy
  • Knowledge, Truth, and Learning
  • Philosophy of Religion and Philosophical Theology
  • Social and Political Philosophy
  • Theology and Biblical Studies

Field names as published on the current Program Design page. VERIFY Academic Office (official field names)

Language

Before your thesis examination, show reading knowledge of one language other than English relevant to your research. An approved language course usually counts as an elective.

Transfer credit

Transfer up to two graduate courses with approval from your mentor, the Registrar and the Academic Dean.
