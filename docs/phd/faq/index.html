PhD questions

Background, study options and applying to the PhD in Philosophy VERIFY Academic Office (official degree name).

For curriculum and degree options, see the programme page.

Is it for me?

Do I need a background in philosophy for the PhD?

No. Philosophy is encouraged, not required. You need a master’s degree in your intended field.

Do I have to want to become a professor?

No. Intellectual, spiritual and professional reasons for doctoral study are welcome too.

How you would study

Do I have to move to Toronto?

No. The 2026–27 seminars meet online, live, at weekly times in Eastern Time (Toronto). The conjoint PhD adds a residency in Amsterdam.

Can I study part-time?

Yes, from the start. If you work full-time, ICS recommends one course a term. Plan your pace with your mentor.

Can I take a course before applying?

Yes. Take a couple of courses for credit; they can later count toward a degree. Ask the Academic Registrar for a registration form.

Can I study from outside Canada? What about visas?

Yes. The 2026–27 seminars are online.

ICS cannot currently offer student visas; international students study online. VERIFY Academic Office (current international-student and visa wording) — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION

The degree itself

How long does the PhD take?

About five years full-time: two of coursework, three of dissertation work. Part-time takes longer. See the PhD pathway.

What are the language requirements?

Reading proficiency in at least two languages other than English. You develop it during the degree; it is not required for admission.

Do I have to spend time in Amsterdam for the conjoint PhD?

Yes. Plan for a six-month VU residency; do not assume a waiver. The dissertation defence is in Amsterdam. VERIFY VU agreement (exact residency wording) — VERIFY AGAINST THE CURRENT ICS–VU AGREEMENT

Can courses from elsewhere count?

Yes. Up to two Toronto School of Theology courses can count. ICS also accepts up to two suitable transfer credits, excluding courses used for a completed degree.

Applying and cost

How should I prepare to apply?

Start with your research question and a possible supervisor. Write to the Program Director, then follow the application checklist.

No master’s thesis? Ask about your preparation before ruling yourself out. VERIFY Academic Office (is an MA thesis required for PhD admission?)

What does it cost, and is there financial aid?

Current costs are on the tuition and fees page.

Ask the Registrar about aid. Decisions have historically been annual, for students admitted by the deadline. VERIFY Academic Office (current financial-aid process and any scholarships)

When is the application deadline?

February 1, for fall admission. VERIFY Academic Office (current application deadline) See how to apply.

Late applications are considered individually, usually without financial aid. VERIFY Academic Office (late applications and aid)
