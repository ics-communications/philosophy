PhD questions

Plain answers to the questions people ask most about the PhD in Philosophy VERIFY Academic Office (official degree name).

Not answered here? Write to Neal DeRoo, the Program Director.

Is it for me?

Do I need a background in philosophy for the PhD?

A background in philosophy is encouraged but not required. What matters most is a master’s-level preparation in your intended area and a question you are ready to pursue.

Do I have to want to become a professor?

No. Many doctoral students prepare for research and teaching, and the program serves that vocation well. Others come for intellectual, spiritual, vocational or other professional reasons. Both are welcome.

How you would study

Do I have to move to Toronto?

Every MA and PhD seminar in the 2026–27 catalogue meets online, live, at a set weekly time. Times are Eastern Time (Toronto).

ICS is based in Toronto and affiliated with the Toronto School of Theology, and its conjoint PhD is with the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. These are academic connections, not places you have to live.

Can I study part-time?

Yes. You can study part-time from the start, including during coursework. A part-time pathway takes longer; plan it with the Program Director.

Can I study from outside Canada? What about visas?

Yes. Because every MA and PhD seminar in the 2026–27 catalogue meets online, you can take the programs from outside Canada.

ICS cannot currently offer student visas, so international students study online. VERIFY Academic Office (current international-student and visa wording) — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION

All programs require proficiency in English; ICS’s international students page gives the details.

Can I take a course before applying?

You can take a couple of courses for credit before committing to a program, and they can later count toward the degree. You are welcome to take courses for credit without pursuing a degree. Write to the Academic Registrar for a course registration form.

The degree itself

How long does the PhD take?

On the full-time pathway, about two years of coursework followed by about three years of dissertation work. You can study part-time from the start, including during coursework. A part-time pathway takes longer; plan it with the Program Director.

What are the language requirements?

Reading proficiency in at least two languages other than English, required to complete the degree. You do not need them to be admitted. Students build this competence during the program.

Do I have to spend time in Amsterdam for the conjoint PhD?

Coursework and examinations are completed with ICS; the dissertation defence and the conferral of the degree take place at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.

The conjoint agreement formally includes a six-month residency requirement at the VU. Plan for it; do not assume it will be waived. VERIFY VU agreement (exact residency wording) — VERIFY AGAINST THE CURRENT ICS–VU AGREEMENT

What is the candidacy examination?

A written and oral examination before internal and external examiners. It tests your readiness to begin dissertation research and your mastery of the broad areas your proposed dissertation engages.

Can courses from elsewhere count?

Up to two Toronto School of Theology courses can count toward your degree. ICS accepts up to two credit transfers for courses at the right academic level that fit your program, but not from a program for which you already received a degree.

Applying and cost

How should I prepare to apply?

A master’s degree in your intended area of study. A background in philosophy is encouraged but not required.

Whether your master’s degree must include a thesis is being confirmed. If yours did not, write to the Program Director before deciding not to apply. VERIFY Academic Office (is an MA thesis required for PhD admission?)

Before applying, write to the Program Director about your question and the faculty member you might work with. A complete application includes writing samples, transcripts and letters of reference. The second page of the application form lists everything.

What does it cost, and is there financial aid?

ICS keeps tuition affordable. Current fees are on the tuition and fees page.

Financial-aid decisions have historically been made once a year, for students admitted by the annual deadline. Ask the Academic Registrar what assistance is currently available. VERIFY Academic Office (current financial-aid process and any scholarships)

When is the application deadline?

February 1, for fall admission. VERIFY Academic Office (current application deadline)

Late applications are considered case by case but are not usually eligible for financial aid. VERIFY Academic Office (late applications and aid)
