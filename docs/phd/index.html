PhD in Philosophy VERIFY Academic Office (official degree name)

Develop an original contribution to Christian scholarship in philosophy, theology or biblical studies. Begin with a research question and a faculty member whose work meets it.

Full-time
About five years: two of coursework, three of dissertation
Part-time
Available from the start; plan your pace with your mentor

Find a prospective mentor

Your faculty mentor guides course choices and dissertation research. Read their work to see where your question connects.

  • Neal DeRoo

    How spiritual forces, not only social and political ones, shape the way we experience the world, and what that means for politics, culture and the church.

  • Nik Ansell

    Biblical and systematic theology that stays connected to lived experience: Christology, eschatology, wisdom, and the theology of gender.

  • Ronald A. Kuipers

    What faith can mean in a secular, pluralistic age, worked out with pragmatism, hermeneutics and critical theory.

From coursework to dissertation

Coursework
Foundation courses, the Interdisciplinary Seminar and electives, chosen with your mentor.
Proposal
A literature review develops into your dissertation proposal.
Candidacy
Written and oral examinations test your readiness for dissertation research.
Languages
Reading proficiency in at least two languages besides English, developed during the degree.
Dissertation
Original research, writing and an oral defence.

Seminars meet online, live, at weekly times in Eastern Time (Toronto). Plan your course load around those meetings.

ICS PhD

Granted by ICS under Ontario authority, with the dissertation defence in Toronto.

ICS–VU conjoint PhD

Coursework and examinations at ICS; dissertation defence and degree conferral at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (VU).

Discuss your research question

Write to Neal DeRoo, the Program Director, with your question, your academic background and the faculty member you hope to work with.

nderoo@icscanada.edu

Before you apply

A master’s degree in your intended field and proficiency in English are required. Philosophy preparation is encouraged, but not required.

Whether your master’s degree must include a thesis is being confirmed. If yours did not, write to the Program Director before deciding not to apply. VERIFY Academic Office (is an MA thesis required for PhD admission?)
