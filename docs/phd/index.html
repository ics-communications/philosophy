Pursue Your Question in Depth

The PhD in Philosophy VERIFY Academic Office (official degree name) is for people ready to make an original contribution to Christian scholarship, whether they are preparing for research and teaching in a university or pursuing doctoral study for intellectual, spiritual, vocational or other professional reasons.

Who the PhD is for

  • Future professors and researchers preparing for an academic vocation.
  • Writers, pastors, educators, institutional leaders and public intellectuals who want doctoral depth behind their work.
  • Working professionals who want to study part-time, including during coursework.
  • Students of theology and biblical studies as well as philosophy: the fields are interdisciplinary.

Two ways to earn the degree

The ICS PhD

The ICS PhD is granted by ICS under the authority of the Province of Ontario. ICS has granted its own PhD since 2005, under the Institute for Christian Studies Act, 1983 (amended 1992 and 2005). You complete coursework, examinations and the dissertation defence with ICS.

The conjoint PhD with the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam

Coursework and examinations are completed with ICS; the dissertation defence and the conferral of the degree take place at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.

The Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam

Coursework, candidacy, dissertation

  1. Coursework

    Foundation courses, the Interdisciplinary Seminar and electives, chosen with your mentor. Up to two may be Toronto School of Theology courses, and up to two courses can transfer from elsewhere.

  2. Literature review and proposal

    A pre-dissertation literature review that becomes your formal dissertation proposal.

  3. Candidacy examination

    A written and oral examination before internal and external examiners. It tests your readiness to begin dissertation research and your mastery of the broad areas your proposed dissertation engages.

  4. Languages

    Reading proficiency in at least two languages other than English, required to complete the degree. You do not need them to be admitted.

  5. Dissertation and defence

    Research, writing and an oral defence: in Toronto for the ICS PhD, in Amsterdam for the conjoint degree.

The full-time pathway: about five years

  1. About 2 years Coursework, the literature review and proposal, languages, and the candidacy examination.
  2. About 3 years Dissertation research, writing and oral defence.

Part-time. You can study part-time from the start, including during coursework. A part-time pathway takes longer; plan it with the Program Director.

Online, with one residency to plan for

Every MA and PhD seminar in the 2026–27 catalogue meets online, live, at a set weekly time. Times are Eastern Time (Toronto). If you work full-time, ICS recommends one course a term.

ICS is based in Toronto and affiliated with the Toronto School of Theology, and its conjoint PhD is with the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. These are academic connections, not places you have to live.

A mentor for the whole journey

At ICS, students are called Junior Members and faculty are Senior Members. The names say what the relationship is: learning here is apprenticeship, not transaction. Each PhD Junior Member works with a Senior Member mentor, who guides course choices and, in time, the dissertation. Supervision is the heart of the degree, so the best way to start is to find the Senior Member whose work meets your question.

Admission

A master’s degree in your intended area of study. A background in philosophy is encouraged but not required.

Whether your master’s degree must include a thesis is being confirmed. If yours did not, write to the Program Director before deciding not to apply. VERIFY Academic Office (is an MA thesis required for PhD admission?)
