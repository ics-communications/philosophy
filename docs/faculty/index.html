Full-time Senior Members in the MA and PhD

At ICS, students are called Junior Members and faculty are Senior Members. The names say what the relationship is: learning here is apprenticeship, not transaction.

Also teaching MA and PhD seminars this year

These seminars are open to MA and PhD Junior Members but are taught by emeritus or sessional faculty, not by full-time Senior Members.

  • Reconsidering Kant’s Aesthetics

    Rebekah Smick Emeritus faculty

    Fall 2026 Thursdays 10 am–1 pm ET 120107 / 220107

    Kant’s Critique of Judgment re-read against the art theory before it and the place of the aesthetic in premodern ethics and theology.

  • Attention in the Age of Distraction

    Jacob Benjamins Sessional faculty

    Fall 2026 Thursdays 6–9 pm ET 223501

    Attention as a way of inhabiting the world: its phenomenology, the attention economy, and contemplative practices that resist digital fragmentation.

  • Albert the Great, Meister Eckhart, and Women’s Spirituality

    Robert Sweetman Emeritus faculty

    Winter 2027 Thursdays 10 am–1 pm ET 220409

    Eckhart’s mystical discourse against the Dionysian tradition of Albert the Great and Aquinas, and the women’s mysticism of Marguerite Porete.
