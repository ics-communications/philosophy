Apply to the MA or PhD

Send your completed application to the Academic Registrar.

February 1, for fall admission. VERIFY Academic Office (current application deadline)

Prepare and send

Open the MA/PhD application form (PDF) VERIFY Academic Registrar (current MA/PhD application form)

  1. 1 Get the form

    Use the MA/PhD form. Page 2 gives the complete checklist.

  2. 2 Gather your documents

    Include writing samples, transcripts and reference letters. Follow the form’s instructions.

  3. 3 Send your application

    Email your application to academic-registrar@icscanada.edu

Late applications are considered case by case but are not usually eligible for financial aid. VERIFY Academic Office (late applications and aid)

Check your preparation

MA
Four-year bachelor’s degree or equivalent; B+ average. A concentration related to your intended field is preferred.
PhD
Master’s degree in your intended field. Philosophy background encouraged, not required. No master’s thesis? Ask the Program Director about your preparation. VERIFY Academic Office (is an MA thesis required for PhD admission?)
English
English proficiency required. See requirements for international students.
Outside Canada
ICS cannot currently offer student visas, so international students study online. VERIFY Academic Office (current international-student and visa wording) — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION
