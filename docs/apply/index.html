Apply to the MA or PhD

Apply online, then arrange for your transcripts to reach the Academic Registrar. We ask your referees for their letters.

February 1, for fall admission. VERIFY Academic Office (current application deadline)

Prepare and send

Apply online Download the PDF form instead VERIFY Academic Registrar (current MA/PhD application form)

  1. 1 Prepare your documents

    Write a statement of purpose of 500 to 1,000 words and choose an academic writing sample, preferably a research paper of 10 to 20 pages.

  2. 2 Apply online

    Complete the form below and pay the CAD $90 application fee (CAD $120 after the deadline). Paying sends your application.

  3. 3 Arrange transcripts

    Ask each post-secondary institution to send an official transcript directly to academic-registrar@icscanada.edu. We email your referees a link to submit their letters, and let you know as each one arrives.

Late applications are considered case by case but are not usually eligible for financial aid. VERIFY Academic Office (late applications and aid)
