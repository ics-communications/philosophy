Applying to the MA or PhD

Applications go to the Academic Registrar. Before you apply, it often helps to write to the Program Director about what you hope to study.

Questions about applying? Write to the Academic Registrar.

Four steps

  1. 1 Talk about fit

    Write to Neal DeRoo, the Program Director, about your interests and the faculty member you might work with.

  2. 2 Get the form

    Download the MA/PhD application form. Its second page lists everything a complete application includes.

    MA/PhD application form (PDF) VERIFY Academic Registrar (current MA/PhD application form)

  3. 3 Gather the pieces

    A complete application includes writing samples, transcripts and letters of reference. The second page of the application form lists everything.

  4. 4 Send it to the Registrar

    Send your application to the Academic Registrar by the deadline.

    Deadline: February 1, for fall admission. VERIFY Academic Office (current application deadline)

Late applications are considered case by case but are not usually eligible for financial aid. VERIFY Academic Office (late applications and aid)

Admission requirements

MA in Philosophy VERIFY Academic Office (official degree name)
A four-year bachelor’s degree or its equivalent, preferably with a concentration related to your intended field at ICS, and an overall average of B+.
PhD in Philosophy
A master’s degree in your intended area of study. A background in philosophy is encouraged but not required. Whether your master’s degree must include a thesis is being confirmed. If yours did not, write to the Program Director before deciding not to apply. VERIFY Academic Office (is an MA thesis required for PhD admission?)
English
All programs require proficiency in English; ICS’s international students page gives the details.
From outside Canada
Because every MA and PhD seminar in the 2026–27 catalogue meets online, you can take the programs from outside Canada. ICS cannot currently offer student visas, so international students study online. VERIFY Academic Office (current international-student and visa wording) — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION

Ways to study

Full-time
Full-time study means six semester courses a year, for example three in the fall and three in the winter.
Part-time
Both degrees can be taken part-time. If you work full-time, ICS recommends one course a term.
Before you commit
You can take a couple of courses for credit before committing to a program, and they can later count toward the degree. You are welcome to take courses for credit without pursuing a degree. Write to the Academic Registrar for a course registration form.
Credit transfer
ICS accepts up to two credit transfers for courses at the right academic level that fit your program, but not from a program for which you already received a degree.

Tuition and financial aid

ICS keeps tuition affordable. Current fees are on the tuition and fees page.

Financial-aid decisions have historically been made once a year, for students admitted by the annual deadline. Ask the Academic Registrar what assistance is currently available. VERIFY Academic Office (current financial-aid process and any scholarships)
