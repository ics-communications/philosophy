Postdoctoral Researchers

Two postdoctoral researchers are currently based at ICS, each holding an individual Postdoctoral Research Fellowship from the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada. They also teach as sessional faculty.

2026–27 · two SSHRC Postdoctoral Research Fellows

These are individual, externally funded fellowships. ICS does not currently offer a postdoctoral program of its own. VERIFY Program Director (postdoctoral wording)

Andrew Tebbutt PhD, MA, MA, BA

SSHRC Postdoctoral Research Fellow
Phenomenology, Social and Political Philosophy

How our ethical and religious formation shapes the way we take part in public dialogue and live with pluralism, drawing on phenomenology and German idealism.

Full profile on the faculty site

Andrew Tebbutt completed a PhD in Religion at the University of Toronto and holds MAs in Philosophy from the Institute for Christian Studies and Brock University. He was Assistant Professor of Philosophy at Trinity Christian College until the college closed, has taught at ICS as a sessional lecturer in the MA/PhD program, and served as Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Centre for Philosophy, Religion and Social Ethics, where he hosted the Critical Faith podcast.

Research

  • Hegel and German idealism
  • Phenomenology and existentialism
  • Contemporary social and political philosophy
  • Religious language, public dialogue, and pluralism

Jacob Benjamins PhD, MAS, MA, MA

SSHRC Postdoctoral Research Fellow
Phenomenology of Religion and Contemplative Traditions

The philosophy of attention, contemplative traditions and the philosophy of technology, approached through the phenomenology of religion and a theology of creation’s goodness.

Full profile on the faculty site

Jacob Benjamins was previously a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at St. Michael’s College, University of Toronto, on the Templeton project The Metaphysics of Contemplation. He completed a co-doctorate with KU Leuven and Australian Catholic University, and teaches as an adjunct in Martin Luther University’s Christianity, Interfaith Dialogue and Community Engagement program.

Research

  • Phenomenology of religion
  • Theology of creation’s goodness
  • Contemplative traditions and philosophy
  • Constructive theology
  • Philosophy of attention
  • Philosophy of technology

Books

  • The Play of Goodness: Creation, Phenomenology, and Culture (Fordham, 2025)
  • The Act of Contemplation: Metaphysics, Phenomenology and the Religious Life (University of Toronto Press, forthcoming), co-edited
